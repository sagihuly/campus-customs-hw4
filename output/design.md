# Problem 10: Style the website.

I chose a modern, clean design based on Yale's colors, mainly dark blue and white, so that the site would feel connected to the university and give customers the sense that the store is reliable and professional. I also wanted to keep the design a little younger and more interesting, and not make it look like a typical university website.

I improved the fonts, headings, and information hierarchy so that the site would be easy to understand and navigate. In addition, I chose to display the products with large images, clear product cards, and subtle hover effects, so that the products would look more appealing and encourage customers to look at more products and maybe even buy.

I added subtle animations and transitions to make using the site more pleasant, and I matched the chat design to the rest of the site so that it would feel like a natural part of the store and not like an external add-on. My goal is for customers to feel comfortable using the chat and asking questions that help them find products.

In addition, I made sure that the site is also suited for mobile, including the menu, images, product cards, and chat. This is important to me because many customers browse stores on their phones, and I wanted it to be easy for them to find products and navigate the site on small screens too, without struggling or giving up on a purchase. I began the mobile adaptation already in Problem 9, and in this task I kept the same design language on mobile.
